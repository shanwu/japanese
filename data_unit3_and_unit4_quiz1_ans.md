【一、字彙測驗 Vocabulary】

(A) 中翻英
1. 銀行: bank
   解說：bank 是銀行。

2. 圖書館: library
   解說：library 是借書、看書的地方。

3. 醫院: hospital
   解說：hospital 是看醫生的地方。

4. 超級市場: supermarket
   解說：supermarket 是買食物和日用品的地方。

5. 博物館: museum
   解說：museum 是展示藝術、歷史的地方。

6. 郵局: post office
   解說：post office 用來寄信、寄包裹。

7. 書店: bookstore
   解說：bookstore 是買書的地方。

8. 大象: elephant
9. 斑馬: zebra
10. 無尾熊: koala
11. 獅子: lion
12. 烏龜: turtle
   解說：以上都是動物名稱。

13. 鈴鐺: bell
14. 聖誕樹: Christmas tree
15. 聖誕老人: Santa Claus
   解說：這三個都和聖誕節有關。

【二、文法選擇題 Grammar】

31. Where are you going?
   答案：B are
   解說：you 要配 are。

32. I am going to the bank.
   答案：A am
   解說：I 一定配 am。

33. Where is Fiona going?
   答案：C is
   解說：Fiona 是單數。

34. She is going to the park.
   答案：C is

35. Where are they going?
   答案：B are
   解說：they 是複數。

36. They are going to the library.
   答案：B are

37. Where is he going?
   答案：C is

38. We are going to the zoo.
   答案：B are

39. How many lions are there?
   答案：B lions
   解說：how many 後面一定是複數。

40. There's one lion.
   答案：A one

41. There are nine lions.
   答案：B nine

42. Does she like horses?
   答案：A like
   解說：does 後面動詞用原形。

43. Yes, she does.
   答案：B does

44. No, she doesn't.
   答案：B doesn't

45. Do you like tigers?
   答案：A Do

46. No, I don't.
   答案：A don't

47. Wendy likes monkeys.
   答案：B likes

48. Martin doesn't like lions.
   答案：B doesn't

49. I don't like lions.
   答案：A don't

50. Martin and I like zebras.
   答案：A like
   解說：兩個人是複數。

51. It's on Blue Street.
   答案：B on
   解說：街道用 on。

52. Are the gifts ready?
   答案：A ready

53. Yes, they are.
   答案：B are

54. How's the weather?
   答案：A How's

55. It's a beautiful night.
   答案：A a

56. I live in a cave.
   答案：A in

57. A koala eats leaves.
   答案：B eats

58. A wombat's poop is like a cube.
   答案：B cube

59. How many students are there?
   答案：B are

60. There are three students.
   答案：B are
